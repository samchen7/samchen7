# Yixuan (Sam) Chen
Interactive tools for exploring structured knowledge and personal data.

[yixuanchen.org](https://yixuanchen.org)

**[Plexus](https://plexus.yixuanchen.org)**<br>
Four interactive lineage maps: philosophy, political ideologies, art movements and religious traditions. Relationships between ideas are typed, and maps link to each other where they overlap.<br>
<sub>JavaScript · Canvas</sub>

**[Chord](https://chord.yixuanchen.org)**<br>
Searchable, playable chord reference. Each chord is shown on a keyboard and linked to its extensions, substitutions and common tones on the circle of fifths, a Tonnetz and waveforms.<br>
<sub>React · TypeScript · Tone.js</sub>

**[Morsel](https://morsel.yixuanchen.org)**<br>
Private food journal: take the HERB Food ID quiz, stamp meals with photos, and watch a personal taste map fill in. No social feed.<br>
<sub>React · TypeScript · Supabase · D3</sub>
